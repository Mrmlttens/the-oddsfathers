    Scoring rules

    K.O.T.O. · Accuracy first. Unlimited tracked bets and eligible unique pregame picks. Missing wagers never block points.

    • Weekend rating = 100 × correct / max(5, correct + incorrect) + min(5, correct). Maximum 105 CP. Pending and void picks are excluded. Raw accuracy is shown separately.
    • Every eligible unique pregame prediction counts. A 1-for-1 start rates 21; 5-for-5 rates 105; 8-for-10 and 16-for-20 both rate 85. Live hits count provisionally; misses and corrections can lower the rating.
    • The earliest submitted player/stat/game locks the original target. Alternate lines, opposing sides and duplicate slips cannot multiply credit. Different player stats count separately. Every leg uses its own game kickoff.
    • Winning legs count even after a parlay loses. Adding or deleting a ticket never replaces a registered prediction. Picks submitted after kickoff track without competition credit.
    • Contest weeks run Thursday–Wednesday, Eastern time. Winning the weekly crown requires at least five graded eligible picks. After all predictions finalize, the highest qualified positive rating wins (at least two participants; ties share the title). No extra crown points are added.
    • Season rating is the average of qualifying final weekend ratings, not cumulative hits. A qualifying final weekend requires five graded picks. Season title qualification requires three qualifying final weekends. Pending and under-five weeks are excluded from the season average.
    • Wager size, payout and slip count never affect CP. This friends contest does not adjust for line difficulty. Submission timestamps are recorded here; they are not independent sportsbook receipts.
    • Final ESPN snapshots preserve grades and accept verified stat corrections. Unavailable player stats stay under review. Cash results always require sportsbook confirmation.
